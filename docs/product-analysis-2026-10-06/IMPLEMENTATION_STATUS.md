## Feedback round — 12 October (evening)

- **Less text, less generic UI.** Explanations moved behind ⓘ popovers (`InfoTip`) across the Today card, workout page, onboarding, plan setup, set log and assistant setup. The separate "Why this workout?" card was removed. Icon-circle rows gave way to the app's own language: dot-matrix figures and kit renders. Fixtures use the equipment render grid. Image slots for generated art: [RAUTA_IMAGE_BRIEF.md](../design/RAUTA_IMAGE_BRIEF.md) (6 fixture renders).
- **Replace was slow to open.** Find alternative re-ran the engine's substitution search (~55 ms per call in Debug) four times per redraw, during the opening animation too; the swap ran it once more. Now one search per opening, off the main actor, with a loader; the swap reuses its result.
- **Adding from a workout asked which workout.** Picked exercises (and "Add to workout" on an exercise opened while picking) now go straight into the workout the flow started from; the destination page only appears if that workout can no longer take exercises. `AddingExercisesTests` (3).

## Removed — 10 November

At your request: the Summary coaching tiles (Plan my week, Help me adjust, Weekly review) with their three screens and the RAUTA-menu entries to them; the Today card's missed-session row; the Library's Start here shelf; the onboarding name/age note; the Start today / Start next week choice; the kit chips and the per-row ⓘ and Replace icons on a workout. Settings shows the earlier "RAUTA's brain" row again in place of the Private conversations card. The "Rauta Tests" simulator had disappeared and was recreated.

Then: the warm-up is pared down to the clock, a bar of its steps, the body map and the movement's name ("Jumping jacks"), with the full instruction a tap on the name away. The Today card is one name, one dot-matrix figure (planned minutes, or time so far while a workout runs) and one button; the card itself opens the workout, so the date, "See workout" and "Pick up where you left off" are gone. The reps-left picker is gone from the set log: sets are logged at the plan's reps-left target again, on the phone and from the watch, so load progression keeps working; the "record reps left" advice went with it.

## Feedback round — 11 November

- **Player bar flicker.** Recorded the bar collapsing and expanding frame by frame on the simulator. Three causes: the set timer re-rendered inside the system's morph and crossfaded two readings; the accessory animated its own compact change on top of the system morph, so it re-laid out about half a second after the morph ended; and each placement is a fresh copy whose exercise figure started blank until its cache lookup ran. Fixed: the timers ignore surrounding animations, the accessory has no animation of its own, and `ExerciseMediaView` draws a cached image on its first frame. Re-recorded: the timer stays readable through the whole morph and nothing moves after it.
- **Today card** is hidden while a workout runs; the bottom player stands for it.
- **Week strip:** rest-day moons are drawn in the unlit flame's fill and edge.
- **Adding exercises.** With a workout running, "Choose exercises" from RAUTA's menu adds straight into it. The destination page (no workout running, or Reuse) shows the picked exercises as cards, the upcoming workouts as rows (day in dot-matrix, name, size, "Add"), and "New workout" as the bottom button; its intro sentence is gone.
- **Copy.** Intro sentences and long empty-state messages trimmed across screens (load steps, save preset, records, streak, workout list, library search, alternatives, health step, Summary month card).

Later the same day:

- **No more ⓘ.** Every popover is gone (`InfoTip`, `TermInfoButton` and `TrainingTerms.swift` deleted). Where a line still matters it is visible and short: the warm-up shows the movement with a few words under it ("Arms all the way up. Land softly."), a first-time load says "pick a load", the set log shows "Suggested today: X kg" only when there is a suggestion. The hidden explanations (time breakdown, term definitions, section notes) went with their buttons.
- **Subtitles had no room.** A page subtitle ("1324 exercises", "0 of 3 done", "November") hung under the title by an offset that took no space, so filters or content started on top of it. The title bar now makes room for it on every page that has one.
- **Policies & credits** copy shortened; the facts and the "to be confirmed" licence notes are unchanged.

## Observations outside the scope of this work

- **Simulator responsiveness.** The RAUTA character's Rive animation takes 60–76% of the main thread on the simulator (Debug), and launch spends ~6–7 s in the loader running the on-device model library's static initialisers before app code. Taps there lag by seconds. Measure both on a device in Release before drawing conclusions.
- **Catalogue curation.** Most leverage-machine exercises (chest press, seated row, shoulder press) are curated as complex / specialised-only, so beginners at a full gym get few machines. Worth a review in the `gym-lib-react` decisions.

Not verified anywhere: physical-device model download/inference, Watch, Live Activity, Health, notifications, iCloud across two devices, onboarding button taps (see 4).
